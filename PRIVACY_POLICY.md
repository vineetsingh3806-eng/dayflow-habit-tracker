# Privacy Policy for DayFlow

**Last updated:** October 2026

DayFlow ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed from the ground up with a strict commitment to user privacy. DayFlow is a 100% offline personal habit tracker and goal planner.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

DayFlow does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or user data.

- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, register, or log in to use DayFlow.
- **Local Storage Exclusively:** All habit details, completion histories, streaks, notes, and goals are stored exclusively in a private local database stored on your device.
- **No Network Transmission:** DayFlow does not request or possess the Android `INTERNET` permission. The application cannot communicate with any remote servers, cloud infrastructure, or third-party endpoints.
- **No Tracking or Analytics:** DayFlow contains no analytics frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Google Analytics), no telemetry tools, and no crash-reporting SDKs.
- **No Advertising:** DayFlow contains no advertisements, no AdMob, and no Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) integrations.
- **No Artificial Intelligence / External APIs:** DayFlow does not connect to Gemini, OpenAI, or any remote AI service. All progress and streak computations execute on-device.

## 2. Device Permissions

DayFlow requests only the absolute minimum permissions strictly necessary for local device functionality:

- **Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Required on Android 13+ (API 33+) to display local reminder alerts for your scheduled habits.
- **Boot Completed (`android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** Required to re-register your local alarms with the Android `AlarmManager` after device restarts or time-zone adjustments.
- **Exact Alarms (`android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`):** Required to trigger habit reminder notifications precisely at the user-scheduled time.
- **Vibrate (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):** Used to provide gentle vibration feedback when a habit is completed or a reminder fires.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because DayFlow does not store your data on external servers, you have complete control over your data:
- To delete your data, you can clear app storage in Android Settings (`Settings > Apps > DayFlow > Storage > Clear Data`) or uninstall the application.
- Uninstalling DayFlow permanently removes all locally stored habits, logs, and settings from your device.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

DayFlow is a general-audience productivity tool. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted here.
